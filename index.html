Case Study 01

Superstore 2016-2019 Sales Analytics & Margin Optimization

Toolkit: Advanced Excel, Power Query, Dynamic Slicers, RFM Modeling

The Business Problem

Despite steady gross revenue growth, executive leadership noticed operating margins plateauing below 13%. Traditional reporting only tracked overall quarterly top-line numbers, obscuring loss-making transactions and unprofitable regional discounts.

AUDIT HIGHLIGHTS:
Furniture Category Net Margin: 2.4% (Severe Discrepancy)
Tables & Bookcases Subcategory: -$17,725 Negative Net Profit
Highest Yielding Region: West Region ($108.4K Profit)

The Analytical Solution

  • Developed dynamic Excel data model utilizing XLOOKUP and dynamic array FILTER functions.
  • Created customer segment tiers (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office) to correlate discount percentages with reorder rates.
  • Implemented dynamic threshold conditional formatting to flag any order with a discount > 20% eroding product margin.

Toolkit: Power BI, Complex DAX, Geospatial Mapping, Time Intelligence

The Business Problem

KEY DIAGNOSTICS:

Simulated Recoverable Fare: $318,000+ Annual Potential

  • Delivered an operational simulation slicer allowing city leads to model surge-multiplier impacts on ride completion.
Case Study 03

Samsung Supply Chain & Warehouse Dispatch Optimization

Toolkit: Power BI, Power Query (M-Code ETL), Lead-Time Modeling, Star-Schema

The Business Problem

Severe packaging and dispatch backlogs accumulated every Monday and Tuesday, causing expensive worker overtime and delayed delivery SLAs across major retail distributors.

OPERATIONAL METRICS:
Peak Demand Window: Mon-Tue Morning Shift (58% of volume)
Carrier Variance Discrepancy: +1.4 Days over SLA on Regional Hub 2
Post-Implementation Dispatch SLA: 94.8% On-Time Fulfillment

The Analytical Solution

  • Created an end-to-end Power Query ETL pipeline integrating warehouse ERP tables with shipping API manifests.
  • Modeled hourly order volume and dispatch latency using DAX to balance worker shifts against real transaction volume.
  • Equipped operations directors with carrier performance scorecards to hold delivery partners accountable.
